# Pending asset: 0.5 mL PCR-tube holder with integrated optics

The final STEP/STL archive for the 0.5 mL PCR-tube holder and integrated optics
is expected in a later push. The holder is designed for the confirmed optical
configuration of this project:

- 5 mm 485 nm LED on ioRodeo `radial_16mA` board, `ver_0p1_rev_3`
- Neemoo Ex470BP-40 excitation filter, **8 x 8 x 1 mm**
- Promega E4941 thin-walled 0.5 mL PCR tube
- Neemoo Em532BP-40 emission filter, **8 x 8 x 1 mm**
- one Adafruit TSL2591 mounted with its detection axis at 90° to the excitation axis
