# Troubleshooting

## Planned scope

This chapter will provide one compact symptom–cause–action table covering:

- startup, USB-drive, and firmware problems;
- blank, calibration, and JSON-loading problems;
- unexpectedly low, high, unstable, or saturated signals;
- bubbles, fingerprints, tube-positioning, and light-leakage effects; and
- missing or inconsistent CSV records.

Detailed installation-specific troubleshooting remains in Chapter 7 until this consolidated table is completed.
