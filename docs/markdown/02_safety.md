# Safety and Laboratory Requirements

> **Chapter status:** final workshop risk assessment and local waste-disposal rules remain to be added.

## General laboratory practice

Use clean, DNA-free consumables and follow the safety documentation supplied with the Promega QuantiFluor® ONE dsDNA System and DNA standards. Wear suitable gloves and laboratory clothing, avoid contamination of open tubes, and dispose of used consumables according to local rules.

## Instrument safety

Keep liquids away from the PyBadge, USB connector, battery, and internal electronics. Switch the instrument off before opening the enclosure or changing hardware. Use only the documented power sources and inspect cables and the LiPo battery before use.

## Workshop responsibility

The local workshop organiser must provide the applicable risk assessment, safety data sheets, emergency information, and waste stream before practical work begins.
