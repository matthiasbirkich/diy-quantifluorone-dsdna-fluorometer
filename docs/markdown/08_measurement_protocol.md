# 08 — Measurement Protocol

> **Purpose:** Rapid fluorometric determination of dsDNA using the Promega QuantiFluor® ONE dsDNA System and the DIY-QuantiFluorONE-dsDNA-Fluorometer.
> 
> This protocol is intended for participants with previous laboratory experience. All assay reagents are supplied with the kit; no reagent preparation is required.

---

## 1\. Materials and equipment

|Category            |Required items                                                             |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Personal protection |Powder-free latex gloves                                                   |
|Pipettes            |P2 or P10 pipette; 200 µL pipette; 1,000 µL pipette if required            |
|Pipette tips        |Sterile, DNA-free tips suitable for each pipette                           |
|Assay kit           |Promega QuantiFluor® ONE dsDNA System, 100 assays                          |
|Sample vessels      |Thin-walled Promega E4941 0.5 mL PCR tubes                                 |
|Laboratory equipment|Tube rack, drawer or another dark place for incubation                     |
|Instrument          |DIY-QuantiFluorONE-dsDNA-Fluorometer                                       |
|Digital access      |Smart device with access to the GitHub repository and the Calibration Suite|
|Documentation       |Notebook, pencil and permanent marker                                      |
|Incubation (dark)   |Timer                                                                      |

## 2\. Clean working conditions

- Work on a clean bench and wear fresh gloves\.
- Use only sterile, DNA\-free tips, tubes, and other sample\-contact materials\.
- Use a fresh pipette tip for every transfer\.
- Keep open tubes away from the fluorometer, smart device, notebook, and pencil\.
- Replace gloves immediately after possible contact with DNA\-containing material\.

> **Important:** The fluorometer and documentation materials do not need to be sterile, but they must be clean and must not come into contact with open samples or reagents.

## 3\. Assay setup

1. Label one PCR tube for the **blank** and additional tubes for standards, quality\-control samples, and unknown samples\.
2. Mix the QuantiFluor® ONE reagent and DNA solutions gently\. Avoid foaming\.
3. Pipette **200 µL QuantiFluor® ONE reagent** into each tube\.
4. Add **1 µL** of blank solution, standard, quality\-control sample, or unknown sample\.
5. Close each tube and mix gently without creating bubbles\.
6. Keep all prepared tubes protected from direct light for 5 minutes\.
7. Measure inthe fluorometer with cap\.

### Assay volume

|Component                          |Volume    |
|-----------------------------------|---------:|
|QuantiFluor® ONE reagent           |200 µL    |
|Blank, standard, control, or sample|1 µL      |
|**Total assay volume**             |**201 µL**|

## 4\. Incubation

Place the prepared tubes in the tube rack and incubate them in a closed drawer or another dark place\.

- Use the incubation time specified in the Promega kit instructions\.
- Apply the same incubation time to blank, standards, controls, and samples\.
- Start the timer immediately after preparing the last tube, or prepare the tubes in clearly timed batches\.

## 5\. Measurement

1. Switch on the DIY\-QuantiFluorONE\-dsDNA\-Fluorometer\.
2. Open the Calibration Suite on the smart device when calibration data must be loaded, checked, or evaluated\.
3. Select or load the calibration for the current measurement series\.
4. Insert the blank tube and perform the blank measurement\.
5. Measure calibration standards or quality\-control samples when required\.
6. Measure the unknown samples one after another\.
7. Insert each tube in the same orientation and ensure that it is fully seated in the holder\.
8. Record the displayed concentration and accompanying quality information\.

> **Do not measure** tubes with visible bubbles, liquid droplets, or fingerprints in the optical measurement area. Clean or replace the tube before repeating the measurement.

## 6\. Recording and checking results

Record the following information:

- Sample identification
- Date and time
- Calibration used
- Displayed dsDNA concentration
- Measurement error or uncertainty shown by the instrument
- LOD and LOQ shown by the instrument
- Applied dilution factor
- Relevant observations or repeated measurements

### Interpretation

- **Below LOD:** Do not report as a quantified concentration\.
- **Between LOD and LOQ:** Report as detected, but not reliably quantified\.
- **Above the calibration range:** Dilute the sample and measure it again\.
- **Replicate measurements:** Report the mean and note clearly inconsistent results\.

## 7\. Completion

1. Dispose of used tubes and pipette tips according to local laboratory rules\.
2. Clean the working area and wipe the outside of the fluorometer\.
3. Do not allow liquid or cleaning agent to enter the fluorometer\.
4. Store the QuantiFluor® ONE kit components according to the manufacturer’s instructions\.
5. Save the measurement data before closing the Calibration Suite\.

## Quick reference

1. Put on fresh gloves and label the tubes\.
2. Add **200 µL reagent** to each tube\.
3. Add **1 µL blank, standard, control, or sample**\.
4. Mix gently and incubate protected from light\.
5. Blank the fluorometer and load the correct calibration\.
6. Measure standards, controls, and samples\.
7. Record concentration, error, LOD, LOQ, and any dilution\.
