# Filter spectral-verification figures

## Files

- `qfo_ex470bp40_spectral_verification.jpeg`
- `qfo_em532bp40_spectral_verification.jpeg`

## Provenance

The filters were purchased as Neemoo Ex470BP-40 and Em532BP-40 filters from AliExpress item `1005010613836251`.

The supplier provides the nominal filter designation and transmittance information. The installed filter specimens were additionally measured by the project team with a self-built DIY spectrometer based on Leslie Wright's open-source PySpectrometer2 project.

The displayed project curves were normalized independently to the maximum of each measured curve. They are used to verify spectral position, shape, and approximate half-maximum intervals, not to establish absolute transmittance.

## Reported passbands

- Ex470BP-40: approximately 450–490 nm at half maximum.
- Em532BP-40: approximately 518–555 nm at half maximum.

## Recommended captions

**Ex470BP-40:** Spectral verification of the installed Neemoo Ex470BP-40 excitation filter. The project measurement was normalized to its maximum; the approximate half-maximum interval was 450–490 nm.

**Em532BP-40:** Spectral verification of the installed Neemoo Em532BP-40 emission filter. The project measurement was normalized to its maximum; the approximate half-maximum interval was 518–555 nm.
