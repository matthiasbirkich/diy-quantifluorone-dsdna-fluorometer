## Interpretation of the 400 ng/µL point

The first paired result is the separately prepared nominal 400 ng/µL standard used as the upper anchor of the DIY two-point calibration. It returned 395 ng/µL when measured on the Quantus.

This point is shown in the figures but excluded from the independent linear regression. The independent regression therefore uses the remaining 14 validation results.

## Important limitations

- Quantus was used as a commercial comparison instrument, not as a traceable reference method.
- The three TSL2591 readings within one measurement cycle are technical sensor repeats, not independently prepared assay replicates.
- Negative blank-corrected firmware values are displayed and reported as zero or below LOD.
- The primary LOD and LOQ estimates are based on ten independently prepared blank tubes.


## Additional technical-repeat files

The processed-data file `diy_triplicate_concentration_summary.csv` converts the three TSL2591 readings of each measurement cycle into concentration units using the active two-point calibration parameters. It reports:

- the triplicate mean concentration;
- the triplicate SD; and
- the 95% confidence-interval half-width based on n = 3 technical readings.

These values support graphical presentation of the operational two-point-calibrated DIY results, but they remain **technical sensor repeats** rather than independently prepared assay replicates.
